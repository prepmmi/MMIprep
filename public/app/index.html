Think out loud

Interviewers are scoring your reasoning process, not just your final answer — narrate the trade-offs you're weighing as you get to it.

Use the full prep window

A quick outline of 2-3 points before you start talking beats improvising — it keeps your answer structured under pressure.

It's OK to pause

A short pause to collect your thoughts reads as composed, not unprepared. Silence feels longer to you than it does to an interviewer.

Watch your own playback

Re-watching your response after each station is the fastest way to catch filler words, posture, and pacing habits you can't feel in the moment.

Look at the camera, not the screen

Position your webcam near eye level — glancing down at your own video feed reads as broken eye contact on review.

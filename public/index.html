FAQ

Questions applicants actually ask

Is this only for MD/DO applicants?+
MMI shows up in med, dental, pharmacy, PA, and vet admissions — the station format and scoring are the same either way, so it works for any MMI-format interview.
Do I need to know my school's exact stations?+
No — the point of MMI is that stations are novel by design. We rotate you through the same categories (ethics, roleplay, policy, personal) so you're comfortable with the format itself, not memorized answers.
How fast can I start?+
Immediately after checkout — no scheduling, no waiting on a coach's calendar.
What do I need?+
A laptop or phone with a camera and mic, and a quiet 30 minutes per circuit.
